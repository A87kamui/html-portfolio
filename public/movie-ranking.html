The Best Movies Acording to Adrian

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Voices of a Distant Star

When a teenage girl is sent to a distant universe to battle aliens,
her relationship with a boy is carried out through text messages
that take ever longer to reach Earth

Memories (1995)

In the chilling "Magnetic Rose," engineers on a spacecraft board an abandoned space station and encounter disturbing paranormal forces.
A young lab worker accidentally swallows a chemical weapon and becomes a walking killing machine in "Stink Bomb."
In "Cannon Fodder," a young boy and his father fight for survival in a city functioning on paranoia.

The Bourne Identity

A man, salvaged, near death, from the ocean by an Italian fishing boat.
When he recuperates, the man suffers from total amnesia, without identity or background,
except for a range of extraordinary talents in fighting, linguistic skills and self-defense that speak of a dangerous past.
He sets out on a desperate search-assisted by the initially rebellious Marie, to discover who he really is, and why he's being lethally pursued by assassins
